IBeam 2.16.0 adds tenant and customer user management for enterprise onboarding through the Management API. Support users can administer their assigned applications while customer authentication, invitation acceptance and tenant data remain owned by each application backend.

### Highlights

- Two new optional packages: `IBeam.Management.Identity.Target.Api` and `IBeam.Management.Identity.Target.Services`.
- Scoped tenant, customer membership and invitation directories, with bounded queries and protected continuation tokens.
- Enterprise onboarding with a first administrator invitation, target-owned role and onboarding policy discovery, durable progress and optional consumer initialization.
- Tenant creation, conditional rename and enable/disable; invitation-based customer provisioning, resend and revoke; membership status and role administration with last-administrator protection.
- Durable idempotency receipts, conditional versions, reconciliation of uncertain outcomes and metadata-only audit records. Invitations are accepted by verified customer accounts through native Identity.
- Live tenant and membership access enforcement for customer requests and refreshes, and trusted self-service/support-provisioned tenant provenance.
- A portable, NuGet-only Management API bootstrap with three synthetic customer backends, real support/customer MFA, startup scripts and a frontend agent handoff prompt.

### Upgrade and integration

- Keep related IBeam packages aligned at **2.16.0**. Packages target **.NET 10**.
- Register the optional target packages in each customer Identity backend and the corresponding portal services/endpoints in the management host. Configure separate support and customer identity realms, target trust, machine capabilities and support app assignments before enabling customer operations.
- Install native customer tenant/membership access enforcement before advertising status or membership management capabilities. Custom stores must implement the required conditional administration contracts; unsupported capabilities remain unavailable.
- Customer onboarding uses invitations and customer-owned acceptance. It does not give support users customer sessions or access to business tables. Billing, Licensing and Credits remain optional.
- Existing `AddIBeamIdentityApi` registration remains available. Hosts providing their own `IIdentityCommunicationSender` may register it first and use `AddIBeamIdentityApiWithCommunicationSender`.
- The bootstrap pins 2.16.0 and can restore published packages from NuGet.org. A local package feed remains optional for validation. The frontend connects to the running Management API.
- See the [bootstrap](https://github.com/MedPACTech/IBeam/tree/v2.16.0/samples/Management.Bootstrap), [frontend handoff](https://github.com/MedPACTech/IBeam/blob/v2.16.0/samples/Management.Bootstrap/docs/baseline-app-prompt.md), and [management guides](https://github.com/MedPACTech/IBeam/tree/v2.16.0/docs/management).
